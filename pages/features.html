Eterna Features

More than memories — create a living, interactive legacy your family can return to anytime.

Voice Preservation

Record your natural tone, accent, and warmth through casual conversation. Eterna learns how you speak and think.

Interactive Echo

Your loved ones can ask you anything — and get replies in your voice. It’s like you’re still there to guide them.

Memory Timeline

Chronologically explore life’s milestones with voice notes, text, photos and videos — searchable by tag, date, or topic.

Final Messages

Leave goodbye messages or last thoughts for the people who matter most. Deliver them when the time is right.

Family Tree Access Control

Decide who can hear which stories. Echo permissions are linked to your family tree, giving you full control over privacy.

Memory Montages

Auto-generated highlight reels bring together your favourite stories, quotes, and moments — beautifully curated.

Eternal Storage

Choose your archive duration — from a few years to a century. Rest easy knowing your legacy will last beyond lifetimes.

Community & Support

Join Discord groups and connect with others building digital legacies. Share advice, ideas, and support each other’s journeys.

Emotional Guardrails & Healing

Eterna is designed with care — using ethical AI practices, grief-sensitive prompts, and emotion-aware responses to support healing. It’s not just about preserving the past, but offering comfort in the present.
