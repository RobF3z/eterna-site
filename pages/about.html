About Eterna

Eterna is more than technology — it’s a way to preserve your voice, your story, and your legacy for generations to come.

We believe that memories are more than just images or dates — they are conversations, laughter, and the wisdom we pass on. With Eterna, you can record and shape your digital legacy in your own voice, so loved ones can interact with your story long into the future.

Whether you’re a grandparent wanting to share life lessons, a parent preserving family history, or someone who just wants their story remembered — Eterna gives you the tools to save what matters most.

Why We Built Eterna

After losing loved ones ourselves, we were struck by how much we wished we could have just one more conversation. Their stories live on in our memories — but their voices fade. Eterna is our answer to that feeling.

By combining advanced AI with ethical design, we’ve made it possible to preserve and interact with your personal legacy like never before.

Our Mission

To make memory preservation accessible, meaningful, and enduring — for every family, in every generation.
